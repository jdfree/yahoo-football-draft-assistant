The rule that decides this draft

Half a point per completion makes every quarterback look enormous. It actually makes them interchangeable.

Completions pay 0.5 here against a Yahoo default of 0, and passing touchdowns pay 6 against a default of 4. That lifts every starting QB into the 420–540 range — but it lifts the twelfth one nearly as much as the first. Measured as points over the last startable player at each position, quarterback is the flattest position on the board:

Before you trust the projections

Rotowire hands full workloads to backups

A handful of players project far above their consensus rank because the projection assumes a starting role they have not won. Verify the depth chart before spending an early pick on any of these — at their rank they are fine late-round darts, and terrible round-4 picks.

watchJadarian Price (RB-Sea, proj 262, rank 62) · Rashid Shaheed (WR-Sea, proj 237, rank 135) · Kenny Gainwell (RB-TB, proj 217, rank 115) · Parker Washington (WR-Jax, proj 234, rank 57)

Week 14 is stacked against you

Following this board, McBride, Love, Turpin, Aubrey and Prescott all sit on bye in week 14 — the last week before playoffs open in week 15. If you are fighting for seeding, that week is close to unplayable. Break the cluster by taking a week-6 or week-11 alternative at either round 3 or round 11.
